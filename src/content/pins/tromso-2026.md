On our trip through Norway we spent a weekend in Tromsø, and of course we had to stop by the northernmost Hard Rock Cafe in the world.

Picking a pin was as hard as ever. In the end I went with this one over a pin showing the Arctic Cathedral.

Afterwards we treated ourselves to a plate of nachos. I'm not sure they were the original recipe, but they didn't taste as good as I remember. They've always been better, and I hope Hard Rock hasn't changed the recipe everywhere.
