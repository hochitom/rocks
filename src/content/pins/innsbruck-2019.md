Some things just happen by chance. I didn't know there was a second Hard Rock Cafe in Austria besides Vienna, until I suddenly found myself standing in front of it.

After our trip around the world, it was time for a road trip through western Austria and southern Germany. We drove to Innsbruck and spent a few days there, then carried on to Sölden, Bregenz and Lindau, and headed home by way of Neuschwanstein Castle.
