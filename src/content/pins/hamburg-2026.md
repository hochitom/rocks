We came to Hamburg to see Beirut play an open-air show in the Stadtpark, and stayed a few extra days for a short city trip. The weather was proper Hamburg weather: rain, wind and cold.

On our walk along the Landungsbrücken we passed the Hard Rock Cafe, so of course we had to go in. It's the first cafe I have two pins from, and all the way home I was nervous that I had picked the same pin as last time. Luckily, I hadn't.
