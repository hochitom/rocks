How cool can a city's name possibly be? We drove a camper van up Australia's east coast, from Sydney to Cairns. We stopped in Surfers Paradise to see the city and to meet "Bert", who had emigrated from Kindberg and was a friend of my grandparents. We had never met before, but he was happy to see us anyway when we turned up on his doorstep.

And the pin? What else could it be: surfboards, of course.
